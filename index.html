How JanVaani Works

A simple 4-step process to get your voice heard

1. File Complaint

Submit your grievance with details, location, and supporting evidence like photos.

2. Categorization

Your complaint is automatically categorized and routed to the concerned department.

3. Government Action

Officials review and take necessary action. You receive real-time status updates.

4. Resolution

Once resolved, the complaint is marked complete. You can rate the response.

About JanVaani

JanVaani (जनवाणी) means "People's Voice" in Hindi. We are a dedicated public grievance platform connecting citizens of Nallasopara, Virar, and Vasai directly with their local government bodies. Our mission is to ensure every complaint is heard, tracked, and resolved with complete transparency.

With color-coded area statistics, citizens and officials can instantly identify problem hotspots — Red for high complaint areas needing urgent attention, Yellow for moderate issues, and Blue for well-maintained zones.
